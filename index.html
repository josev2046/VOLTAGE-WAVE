Stem Specifications

Recommended Parameters for Hardware Processing

CHANNEL FORMAT

  • ■ Strictly mono (single-channel WAV or AIFF).
  • ■ For stereo sources: Export separate left and right mono stems for independent processing. Maintain identical interface buffer/latency settings during capture to ensure sample-accurate alignment when hard-panning the returns.

STEM DURATION

  • ■ Loops: Because we physically ride the hardware circuitry in real-time, please send 1-to-2 minute evolving loops (16 to 64 bars) rather than full 8-minute track renders.
  • ■ Full Takes: For continuous, non-looping performances (like solos or vocoding), please limit stems to 5 minutes maximum to ensure optimal tactical performance.

GAIN STAGING

  • ■ Peak levels between -6dBFS and -3dBFS. This ensures a robust output without digital clipping prior to conversion.
  • ■ Routing Note: The patch bay `EXT SIGNAL IN` has a 3Vp-p limit and may clip prematurely. We route via the front-panel input for greater headroom, utilising the `EXTERNAL SIGNAL LEVEL` knob to set the precise analogue drive.

PROCESSING & EFFECTS

  • ■ Completely dry: Disable all reverbs, delays, and digital modulation before export.
  • ■ Uncompressed: Bypass all digital compression or limiting. The hardware voltage-controlled amplifier and filter rely on natural volume fluctuations to overdrive interactively.

FREQUENCY CONTENT

  • ■ Full spectrum and raw.
  • ■ Do not apply pre-equalisation. The resonant low-pass filter requires dense, high-frequency harmonic content to process effectively.

FILE FORMAT

  • ■ 24-bit WAV or AIFF.
  • ■ Sample rate matched to the project session (e.g., 48kHz).
  • ■ Strictly lossless to prevent digital compression artefacts from being exaggerated by the hardware's colouration.
