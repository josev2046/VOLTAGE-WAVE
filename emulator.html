HARDWARE EMULATION // KORG MS-10

MS-10 Web Audio

Drag knobs, click switches, or play the keys directly on screen (or use keys A–;). Use ↑ and ↓ arrows for the modulation wheel. Includes all 30 classic Setting Examples and an interactive scope.

This interactive emulator is an educational homage to my very first synthesiser and a love letter to the gritty, screaming sound of the original KORG-35 filter chip. While the routing and logic are functionally identical to the classic 1978 patch sheets, this native Web Audio API approximation will sound noticeably cleaner and tamer than the physical hardware. It lacks the subtle tuning drift of true analogue circuitry, the snappy capacitor-driven envelopes, and the raw analogue distortion of the original units. Audio-rate modulations may also exhibit mild digital aliasing.

For a deep dive into the engineering, signal flows, and history of classic hardware like this, check out my publications at Voltage & Wave.
