About these interactive demos

Each clip is rendered in your browser through a browser-rendered 20-band channel-vocoder approximation informed by the AV-20 signal path: cascaded analysis and carrier filters, full-wave envelope detection, smoothing, and band-wise gain control. The carrier examples use a saw/square/sub-octave keyboard mix where appropriate; the noise-carrier example uses broadband noise. Waveforms and spectrograms are measured from the audio you hear. The voice is a phoneme-level formant synthesiser: deliberately synthetic, with separate voiced, fricative and stop-burst paths. It is not a human recording or a full speech synthesiser. Treatments are exaggerated for orientation.

Understanding the physics: The modulator (voice or drums) acts purely as a set of invisible, multi-band volume knobs for the carrier (synth or noise). When listening to the Hardware Return, you are hearing the continuous carrier being rhythmically sculpted and unmuted by the modulator.
