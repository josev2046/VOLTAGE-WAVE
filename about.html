1st Edition: Errata & Updates

Studio gremlins infiltrated the proofreading phase. Please review these physical hardware and patch routing adjustments:

  • 1. Silencing the VCO: Select Rectangle (Pulse) and turn PW/PWM fully clockwise to mute the internal drone.
  • 2. Input Location: Use the EXT SIGNAL IN jack on the front right patch panel.
  • 3. S-Trigger: Route MIDI through the Kenton's S-Trigger output into TRIG IN.
  • 4. Headroom: Patch directly from your Focusrite line output via a TS cable (skip the re-amp box).
