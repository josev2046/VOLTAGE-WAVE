03 Korg VC-10 — Voces Ex Machina

AN ENGINEER’S MANUAL FOR HARDWARE VOCODING

Two signals. One voice. A vocoder needs two things: something to say, and something to say it with. The VC-10 splits the job: the voice decides what is said; the carrier decides what it sounds like. This is the working engineer’s manual for putting both through the hardware and getting them back.

I N S I D E

  • ▹ Nine protocols in four groups: voice, carrier, pitch and print
  • ▹ Whisper, talking drums, MS-10 and DAW-stem carriers
  • ▹ Gain staging, level arithmetic and bench procedures
