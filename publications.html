03 Korg VC-10 — Voces Ex Machina

AN ENGINEER’S MANUAL FOR HARDWARE VOCODING

Two signals. One voice. A vocoder needs two things: something to say, and something to say it with. The VC-10 splits the job: the voice decides what is said; the carrier decides what it sounds like. This is the working engineer’s manual for putting both through the hardware and getting them back.

I N S I D E

  • ■ Nine protocols in four groups: voice, carrier, pitch and print
  • ■ Whisper, talking drums, MS-10 and DAW-stem carriers, ensemble and SQ-10 pitch CV
  • ■ Gain staging, level arithmetic and bench procedures to measure your own unit
  • ■ Two worked sessions, a patch matrix and session logs

Book 02

02 Korg SQ-10 — Off The Grid

UNQUANTISED STEP-SEQUENCING & MIDI-TO-CV TRANSLATION

The Korg SQ-10 makes no sound. At every step it sends three control voltages, and what a synthesiser does with them is up to you. Set pitch by ear and it lands between the notes a MIDI grid allows. Patch a channel into the clock and the beat pushes and pulls. Drive the filter step by step, or run a DAW stem through the MS-10 while the sequencer moves it. This is the working engineer’s manual for all of it: twelve repeatable procedures, each laid out as an open spread, with the patch on the left, the listening check on the right and a QR code to hear it.

I N S I D E

  • ■ Twelve protocols in four groups: pitch, time, timbre and hybrid stem work
  • ■ Microtonal scales, glides, warped time, polymeter and 24-step phrases
  • ■ Bench procedures to measure your own unit’s voltages, clock and latency
  • ■ Three worked sessions, a patch matrix, tuning tables and session logs
