## The Digital Cure

Modern software synthesisers are incredibly precise, but they lack the unpredictable behaviour and colouration of physical electricity. Voltage & Wave is an analogue bridge for producers who want the aggressive, squelching filters and natural swing of vintage 1970s Korg hardware, without the maintenance or the massive price tag.

Your MIDI and audio stems are physically routed through the studio, sculpted by hand, and captured back into pristine 24-bit digital audio.

## Studio services

- **Analogue Filter Processing & Re-amping:** your flat digital audio, driven through the aggressive, resonant low-pass filters of the vintage Korg MS-10 or Behringer K-2.
- **Authentic Vintage Sequencing:** your MIDI melodies translated by ear onto a 1978 Korg SQ-10. Continuous voltage rather than quantised steps gives your basslines a natural, drifting groove.
- **VC-10 Vocoder Processing:** your vocal and carrier stems blended through the fully restored Korg VC-10, a 20-band analogue vocoder, for warm, robotic textures.
- **Custom Hardware Sound Design:** bespoke stems built from the ground up to your tempo and key, delivered mix-ready.

## How it works

1. **Data Submission:** send your dry audio stems, MIDI sequences or sound design briefs, along with a reference track for the vibe.
2. **Signal Routing:** the studio is physically patched for your chosen path: vintage filters, custom voices, or MIDI-to-CV translation.
3. **Tactile Processing:** no automation. Your tracks are processed in real time as the circuitry is ridden by hand, tweaking filters, envelopes and sequences.
4. **Digital Capture:** receive phase-aligned, mix-ready 24-bit/48kHz WAV files, preserving the unpredictable analogue character.

### Stem specifications

| Requirement | Detail |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Channel format** | Strictly mono (WAV or AIFF). For stereo sources, export separate left and right mono stems and keep interface buffer/latency settings identical during capture for sample-accurate alignment. |
| **Duration** | Loops: 1–2 minute evolving loops (16–64 bars). Full takes (solos, vocoding): 5 minutes maximum. |
| **Gain staging** | Peaks between -6 dBFS and -3 dBFS. |
| **Processing** | Completely dry and uncompressed: no reverb, delay, digital modulation, compression or limiting. |
| **Frequency content** | Full spectrum and raw. No pre-equalisation; the resonant low-pass filter needs dense harmonic content. |
| **File format** | 24-bit lossless WAV or AIFF, at a sample rate matched to your session (e.g. 48 kHz). |

> **Routing note:** the MS-10's patchbay `EXT SIGNAL IN` has a 3 Vp-p limit and can clip prematurely, so stems are routed via the front-panel input for greater headroom, with the `EXTERNAL SIGNAL LEVEL` knob setting the analogue drive.
