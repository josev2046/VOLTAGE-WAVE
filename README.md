## The Digital Cure
Modern software synthesisers are incredibly precise, but they lack the unpredictable behaviour and colouration of physical electricity. We offer an exclusive analogue bridge for producers who want the aggressive, squelching filters and natural swing of vintage 1970s Korg hardware, without the maintenance or the massive price tag. 

Your MIDI and audio stems are physically routed through our studio, sculpted by hand, and captured back into pristine 24-bit digital audio.

## The Hardware Arsenal
Transparency is our guarantee. Here is exactly what is touching your audio:
*   **Korg MS-10 (1978):** Original vintage analogue monosynth, famous for its biting PWM and aggressive filter.
*   **Korg SQ-10 (1978):** Vintage analogue step sequencer (Hz/V). Pure unquantised voltage control.
*   **Hologram Chroma Console:** Boutique outboard multi-effector for tape saturation, fuzz, and modulation.
*   **Focusrite 4i4 (4th Gen) & Palmer DI/Re-Amp boxes:** The transparent, balanced bridge between the digital DAW and physical circuitry.
*   **Kenton Pro Solo Mk3:** Single-channel MIDI to CV/Gate translation layer.
*   **Tape 16 DAW:** Commit-style digital tape machine for focused, destructive recording.

## How It'll Work (Studio calibration is currently in progress while juggling jobs and kids, we expect to launch by Xmas)
1.  **Data Submission:** You send us your dry audio stems, MIDI sequences, or sound design briefs.
2.  **Signal Routing:** We physically patch the studio for your chosen path (filters, sequences, or modulation).
3.  **Tactile Processing:** No automation. Your tracks are processed in real-time as we manually ride the circuitry.
4.  **Digital Capture:** Receive phase-aligned, mix-ready 24-bit/48kHz digital WAV files.
