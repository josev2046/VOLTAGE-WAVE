Off the Grid

The Korg SQ-10 makes no sound. It sends the MS-10 three control voltages per step, with continuous knobs and a free-running clock. Below, all twelve protocols from the book in four groups (pitch, time, timbre and hybrid stem work), each with an exaggerated A/B you can hear.

Audio, waveforms and spectrograms are generated in the browser from the same note data. They illustrate each treatment and are not hardware captures; real captures replace them once recorded. In P-10 and P-11 synth tones stand in for the DAW stem. Items marked CONFIRM ON BENCH are assumptions in the demo, not measured facts.
