01.

EBM Bassline Sweep

Source: Soft-Synth Hardware: Korg MS-10 Process: Filter Re-amp

Engineer Notes: The client provided a very flat, dynamically rigid bassline generated in a DAW. We routed the stem directly into the MS-10's External Signal processor, driving the VCA slightly to introduce harmonic distortion. During the recording pass, the low-pass filter cutoff and peak resonance were manually ridden to follow the groove, bringing human movement to a previously robotic track.

02.

Drum Transient Shaping

Source: Digital Samples Hardware: Palmer DACCAPO / Korg MS-10 Process: VCA Overdrive

Engineer Notes: A rigid, in-the-box techno drum loop lacking cohesion. Instead of using a digital saturator, we re-amped the entire drum bus through the Palmer DACCAPO to achieve correct impedance, then smashed it into the Korg hardware. We bypassed the oscillator and pushed the input volume into the red. The analogue circuitry naturally compressed the sharp digital transients and glued the kit together with authentic noise-floor grit.

03.

Unquantised Sequencing

Source: MIDI File Hardware: Kenton Pro Solo / Korg SQ-10 Process: MIDI to Hz/V

Engineer Notes: The client provided a MIDI melody. Instead of mapping it to a soft-synth, we sent a digital clock pulse out of the interface to sync the Korg SQ-10. We then manually dialed in the pitch voltages for all 12 steps by ear. Because the SQ-10 has no digital quantisation, the resulting sequence naturally "drifts" off the perfect grid, providing an organic, hypnotic pulse impossible to program with a mouse.
